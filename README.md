### 👋 About me

Senior full-stack engineer with **7+ years** in C#/.NET, Angular and Azure.

- 🏛️ **Now:** I lead a three-person team modernizing a state-government HR platform used by **100,000+ employees** (.NET 8/10, Angular 18/20, Kafka, Redis, AKS; **99.5% uptime**). I also own a **30-application ASP.NET Web Forms → Blazor Server** migration.
- 🏥 **Before:** I built claims and payment APIs at a large healthcare company that processed **$25M+ a day**, and proposed the microservices architecture the team adopted.
- 🎯 **What I care about:** systems that have to be right. Auth flows, payments, migrations with no downtime, and the test and CI discipline that keeps them that way.
